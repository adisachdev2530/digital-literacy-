## Introduction
This project focuses on building digital literacy and essential online skills required for
students in today’s technology-driven world. It encompasses practical knowledge of
digital tools, online communication, and cybersecurity awareness. The goal is to
establish a professional digital identity and learn how to use the internet responsibly as
a college student.


## Project Tasks & Implementation
## Task 1: Infographic Creation
Designed a digital infographic titled “Navigating the Digital World” using Canva.

The design presents key topics including Artificial Intelligence, Digital Transformation,
Data Privacy, and the Power of Connectivity.

The infographic highlights essential skills that every student should develop to succeed
in today’s digital world.

## Task 2: Digital Portfolio Setup
Created professional profiles across key platforms to build an online presence:



GitHub: Set up to host assignments and personal projects, building a consistent commit
history.



LinkedIn: Created to build a professional identity, connect with peers and industry
professionals, and track technology trends.




Stack Overflow: Established as a community-driven resource for asking technical
questions and sharing programming solutions.


## Task 3: Platform Exploration & Data Collection

Google Forms: Created a comprehensive “Digital Literacy Awareness Quiz” consisting
of five questions to assess peer understanding of online safety.



Google Sheets: Integrated to automatically capture responses, enabling structured data
analysis and filtering across the batch.


CodeChef: Completed a module test in Strings (C++ and Java), achieving a 66% skill
score to demonstrate core programming logic.

## Task 4: Email Etiquette Case Study
Analyzed the consequences of poor digital communication through a case study of a
student sending an informal, unclear email without a proper subject line.

Highlighted the importance of maintaining proper email etiquette, such as using a clear
subject line and a respectful tone to ensure messages are taken seriously.

## Task 5: Cybercrime Awareness
Researched simple but convincing online scams, particularly phishing and UPI frauds.

Explored how attacks trick users through fake links or messages rather than requiring
advanced technical skills.

Committed to safer digital habits, including verifying sources before clicking unknown
links and avoiding sharing sensitive information.
